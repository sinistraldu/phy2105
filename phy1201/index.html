PHY1201: Physics

Elasticity, oscillations, waves and acoustics, physical optics, and heat and thermodynamics, class by class. Every topic has the theory, step-by-step derivations, worked examples, practice problems, and 2D and 3D simulations whose sliders match the symbols in the equations.

How to use this site

Derive it, see it, solve it

  1. Read the ideaEach class starts with the physical picture, then the key result in a box.
  2. Follow the derivationDerivations are written step by step, so you can reproduce them in an exam.
  3. Play with the simulationsEvery slider is a symbol in the equation. Change one thing at a time and watch the live numbers. 2D views show graphs; 3D views can be rotated, zoomed, paused and slowed down.
  4. Try the problemsWork each problem first, then press Show solution.

Course outcomes

  1. Explain physical quantities and the behaviour of oscillating systems, waves, elastic solids, sound, light, heat and thermodynamic systems.
  2. Demonstrate and analyse the equations of simple, damped and forced harmonic motion, relations among elastic constants, acoustic phenomena, Lissajous figures, the laws of physical optics and of thermodynamics, using analytical and graphical tools.
  3. Evaluate numerical problems on waves, elastic constants, strain energy, acoustics, interference, diffraction, polarization, heat engines, efficiency and COP.
