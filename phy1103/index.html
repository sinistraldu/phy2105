How to use this site

Derive it, see it, solve it

  1. Read the ideaEach class starts with the physical picture, then the key result in a box.
  2. Follow the derivationDerivations are written step by step, so you can reproduce them in an exam.
  3. Play with the simulationsEvery slider is a symbol in the equation. Change one thing at a time and watch the live numbers. 2D views show graphs; 3D views can be rotated, zoomed, paused and slowed down.
  4. Try the problemsWork each problem first, then press Show solution.

Course outcomes

  1. Define and explain the quantities and laws of electricity and magnetism, heat and thermodynamics, and quantum, modern and nuclear physics.
  2. Derive and establish relations such as the field of a dipole, potentials, series–parallel capacitance, the Hall equation, Biot–Savart, Ampère and Gauss laws, Einstein’s photoelectric equation, Compton and de Broglie wavelengths, the decay law, equipartition, work in processes, engine and refrigerator efficiency, entropy, Maxwell’s relations and the uncertainty principle.
  3. Calculate fields, potentials, flux, capacitor energy, degrees of freedom, temperatures, Carnot efficiency, entropy changes, work function and threshold values, half-life, decay constant, activity, uncertainty, phase and group velocity.
