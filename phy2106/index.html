PHY 2106 Physics Laboratory

Interactive experiment guides with easy explanations and 3D virtual labs

Each guide explains the theory, explains in simple words why each formula works, walks through the procedure with the reason behind each step, and includes a virtual lab where you can take readings, draw the graph and calculate the result. Use it before the lab session to prepare and after it to check your calculations.

1

Refractive index of a liquid by plane mirror and pin method

Convex lens on a plane mirror, liquid lens, parallax, spherometer. Live ray tracing.

 2

Acceleration due to gravity by compound bar pendulum

Equivalent length, centres of suspension and oscillation, T against distance graph. 3D swinging bar.

 3

Frequency of a tuning fork by Melde's experiment

Standing waves, transverse and longitudinal arrangements, resonance. 3D string and fork.

 4

Spring constant and effective mass of a spiral spring

Hooke's law, oscillations, effective mass ms/3, T² against M graph. 3D spring.
